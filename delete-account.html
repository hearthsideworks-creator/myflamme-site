Delete your Flamme account

Flamme by Hearthside Works

You can delete your Flamme account and everything you shared at any time. A Flamme account is optional: it's only used for following friends and sharing recipes with them.

Fastest: in the app

  1. Open Flamme and go to the Profile tab.
  2. In the Account card, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm twice. Deletion happens right away.

No longer have the app?

Email hearthside.works@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Flamme account", and include your @handle. Send it from the Google account you signed in with, so we can confirm the account is yours. We'll delete it within 7 days and reply when it's done.

Flamme will never ask for your password - not in the app, not by email. Sign-in only ever happens on Google's own page or pop-up. If a message claiming to be Flamme asks for a password, it isn't us.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile (@handle and display name)
  • The copies of your recipes and photos stored for sharing
  • Your follows, followers, follow requests, blocks and emoji reactions
  • Any recipe share links you created
  • Reports you filed about other accounts
  • Your notification registration

What stays

Recipes saved on your phone are stored only on your phone, so they stay there until you delete them or uninstall the app. Recipes a friend chose to "save a copy" of are now theirs, in their own collection.
