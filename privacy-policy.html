Flamme — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026 · Flamme by Hearthside Works

Flamme is a recipe app. Most of it works entirely on your phone. If you choose to create an account, a small amount of information is stored on our servers so you can follow friends and share recipes with them. This policy explains exactly what, why, and how to delete it.

The short version

  • Without an account, nothing leaves your phone. Recipes, photos, ratings, shopping lists, meal plans and settings stay in the app's private storage.
  • An account is optional. It's only for following friends and sharing.
  • Your email is never shown to anyone — friends see only the @handle and name you choose.
  • Nobody sees your recipes unless you approve them as a follower.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell data.
  • Delete your account any time — in the app, or at myflamme.app/delete-account.

What stays on your phone

Everything you create in Flamme is saved in the app's private storage on your device: recipes, photos, ratings, notes, shopping list, meal plans, Cooking Together plans and settings. If you uninstall the app, that data is deleted with it. We can't see it.

If you create an account

You sign in with Google. Flamme has no passwords and never asks for one.

What we store, and why:

InformationWhy
Your email address and Google account IDTo recognise you when you sign in. Never shown to other people, never used for marketing.
Your @handle and display name (you choose them)So friends can find and recognise you.
The recipes you share: name, ingredients, directions, times, course, links, and whether you favorited or hid themSo the friends you approved can see them. Ratings, scaling and notes stay on your phone.
Photos you add to shared recipesSo friends can see them. Every photo is redrawn and stripped of all hidden data — location, phone model, date and time — on your phone before upload.

Reports you fileSo we can review abuse. The person you report isn't told who reported them.

What we don't keep: Google also shares your full name and profile picture when you sign in. Flamme deletes them automatically at every sign-in. We never collect your location, contacts, or anything from other apps.

Who can see your information

  • People you approve as followers can see your shared recipes, photos, favorites and your @handle and display name. They can't see recipes you mark "Hidden from followers".
  • Anyone you haven't approved can only find you by typing your exact @handle, and sees just your handle, display name and profile picture (if you set one) — no recipes.
  • People you block can't find you, follow you or see anything of yours.
  • Recipe share links you create can be opened by anyone who has the link, and you can turn a link off at any time.
  • Our team can access stored data only to keep the service running, investigate reports, or when required by law.

These rules are enforced by the database itself, not just by the app.

Opening links and pictures from friends

Links inside a friend's recipe always ask before opening, and show the real website. Pictures in a friend's recipe only load from Flamme's own storage or from the same website as the recipe, so other sites can't track you through images.

Importing recipes

When you paste a link to import a recipe, the app fetches that web page directly, the same way your browser would. Nothing about you is added to the request.

Services we use

Flamme relies on a few companies to run. They process data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase — stores account data, shared recipes and photos (servers in the United States).
  • Google — sign-in. Google's own privacy policy applies to your Google account.
  • Expo — delivers app updates and push notifications.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivers push notifications to Android phones.
  • Cloudflare and GitHub Pages — host the myflamme.app website and route support email.

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS).

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep account data for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your profile, shared recipes, photos, follows, followers, reactions, share links, reports you filed and notification token are deleted from our servers immediately. Recipes saved on your phone stay there. Copies of a recipe that a friend chose to save are theirs.

To delete: Profile → Account → Delete account in the app, or sign in at myflamme.app/delete-account.

Children

Flamme isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how data is handled, we'll update this page and announce the change in the app before it takes effect.
